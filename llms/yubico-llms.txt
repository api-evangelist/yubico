# Yubico

> Yubico is the maker of the YubiKey hardware authentication device and the inventor of the Yubico OTP. Its public developer surface is the YubiCloud OTP validation REST API (api.yubico.com/wsapi/2.0/verify) plus first-party SDKs and libraries for WebAuthn/FIDO2, PIV, OATH, OTP, and YubiHSM across Python, .NET, Java, Android, iOS, and C, and the ykman CLI.

## APIs
- [YubiCloud OTP Validation API](https://developers.yubico.com/Software_Projects/YubiCloud_REST_API.html): Verify a Yubico OTP via a signed HTTPS GET to api.yubico.com/wsapi/2.0/verify.
